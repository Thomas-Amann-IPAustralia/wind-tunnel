## 7.1

Yes. Based on the current project outline and threshold assessment, the Policy Impact Mapper is restricted to unclassified internal policy documents and public legislation. While the tool is not intended to process sensitive personal information, internal documents and metadata (such as owner names, status, and internal dates) are ingested, which may contain minor personal data like staff contact details. To ensure compliance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), particularly APP 3 (collection) and APP 6 (use/disclosure), the agency must implement data minimisation techniques, such as reviewing and filtering out unnecessary personal information or de-identifying data before ingestion (OAIC AI Training Checklist, p.2). Furthermore, security measures must be aligned with APP 11 to protect the ingested document repository from unauthorised access or disclosure (OAIC AI Selection Checklist, p.2).
