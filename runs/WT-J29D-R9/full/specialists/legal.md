## 9.1

No. The current project outline does not specify whether individuals, groups, or organisations will be notified if an administrative action (such as the registration of their website as problematic) is materially influenced by the AI system. Under administrative law and best practice, agencies should ensure that notice is provided to affected individuals before or at the time a decision is made, particularly when automated or AI-assisted systems are used to make decisions that materially impact their legal or other significant rights.

*Citations: [ADM Better Practice Guide, p. 42]*

## 9.2

No. The outline does not identify any timely or accessible process for affected parties to challenge the administrative actions or registrations influenced by the AI system. Establishing clear review options and dispute resolution mechanisms is a core requirement of administrative law and contestability principles to ensure that decisions can be reviewed for legality, fairness, and reasonableness.

*Citations: [ADM Better Practice Guide, p. 10], [ADM Better Practice Guide, p. 57]*

## 12.1

No. The specific legislative authority and regulatory instruments governing the identification and registration of 'problematic' websites are not documented in the current outline. Scoping must include a detailed examination of the enabling legislation to ensure the AI system operates within lawful boundaries, has clear statutory authorization, and does not impermissibly constrain administrative discretion.

*Citations: [ADM Better Practice Guide, p. 12], [ADM Better Practice Guide, p. 28]*

## 12.2

Yes. The threshold assessment identifies a clear need for legal advice, recommending a legal audit of the correctness of legal interpretation and validation of data security and privacy. However, because the project is in its early stages, no formal legal advice has been obtained yet, and thus no storage repository (such as a document management system or case file) has been established. Seeking external legal advice on the specific risks of automation in the agency's statutory context is highly recommended before proceeding.

*Citations: [ADM Better Practice Guide, p. 12], [ADM Better Practice Guide, p. 49]*
