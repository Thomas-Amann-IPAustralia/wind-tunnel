⚠️ Prototype Limitations & Sandbox Notice

This is an interactive proof-of-concept demonstrating the Workflow Discovery AI Agent interface. It runs entirely in your browser with simulated logic. Please note the following limitations:

  • No Real AI Processing: The conversational agent, "solutioning" detection, and "no-interpolation" clarification prompts are pre-scripted simulations.
  • No Real Database or Integrations: No data is saved to a server, and it does not connect to Slack, internal wikis, or real document repositories.
  • Simulated Timestamps: The SME validation timestamps are generated locally in your browser session and do not write to an immutable audit log.
  • Static Process Map: The visual workflow diagram is a pre-rendered SVG illustration, not dynamically generated from the chat text.
