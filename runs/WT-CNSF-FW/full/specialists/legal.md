## 9.1

Not applicable. The Departmental Knowledge Graph is an internal tool designed for knowledge management, information discovery, and policy research by departmental staff and policy researchers. It is not designed or used to make or materially influence administrative decisions that have a legal or significant effect on individuals, groups, organisations, or communities. As such, notification requirements under administrative law for AI-influenced decisions do not apply to this use case.

## 12.2

Yes. Given the high inherent risks identified in the threshold assessment—particularly around privacy (3.5) and data security (3.6) due to the ingestion of sensitive unstructured data like email archives—the need for legal advice has been clearly identified (12.2.1). Legal advice is required to ensure compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), confidentiality obligations, and public sector record-keeping requirements. Regarding 12.2.2, as the project is currently in the conceptual/assessment phase, no formal legal advice has been obtained yet, and therefore no storage location (such as a document management system or case file) has been established. Once obtained, any legal advice will be stored in the department's official records management system in accordance with standard information security protocols.
