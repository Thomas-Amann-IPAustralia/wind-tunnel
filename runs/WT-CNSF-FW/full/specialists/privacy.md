## 7.1

No. Based on the current project outline, we are not satisfied that the collection, use, or disclosure of personal information complies with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). The proposed Departmental Knowledge Graph plans to ingest unstructured communications, including email archives and internal wikis, which contain personal and potentially sensitive information. Under APP 3, collecting personal information for AI training or processing is not considered 'reasonably necessary' if the objective can be achieved using de-identified data or a lesser amount of personal information (APP Guidelines, p.91). Furthermore, using existing internal communications for a secondary AI-related purpose (extracting nodes and edges) may violate APP 6, as it is unlikely to align with the primary purpose of collection or the reasonable expectations of the individuals (OAIC AI Training Top 5, p.1). Sensitive information, which is highly likely to be present in email archives, generally requires express consent for collection under APP 3.3 (APP Guidelines, p.91). To achieve compliance, the project must implement robust data minimisation, de-identification, and consent frameworks before proceeding.

*Citations: [APP Guidelines, p.91], [OAIC AI Training Top 5, p.1]*
