No. The current project outline does not identify any established processes or systems for recordkeeping and documentation. Under the Archives Act 1983, the agency has strict record-keeping obligations to ensure transparency and accountability (Archives Act 1983, s 2A). Because this agentic system performs iterative analysis and generates policy recommendations with potentially minimal human oversight, a high level of information management rigour is required (NAA AI Records Guidance, §Managing AI technologies incorporated into business systems). The agency must capture and manage AI-generated outputs (such as synthesized reports and recommendations), system inputs (including user queries, prompts, and source datasets), and system design documentation (NAA AI Records Guidance, §What are the types of AI records?). To achieve this, the project team should conduct a Business System Assessment Framework (BSAF) assessment to ensure that the platform or an integrated records management system can securely and reliably retain these records in accordance with relevant records authorities (NAA AI Records Guidance, §Managing AI technologies incorporated into business systems; NAA AI Records Guidance, §Example: AI technologies incorporated into a regulatory compliance assessment business system).
